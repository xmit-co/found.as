How it works

It takes about a minute. Choose your web address — found.as/yourname — pick a password, add your links, and you're online. Want to change something later? Come back with your password and edit anytime. There's no password reset, so save the recovery kit the editor offers: a little file with everything you need to get back in, ready for your password manager or a drawer.

What you can add

Add the ways people already reach you: your phone, your email, your website, and the messaging and social apps you use every day, like WhatsApp and Instagram. You don't need to know how links work — type your username or paste whatever you have, and found.as turns it into a tidy button. Then drag them into any order, rename them, or hide the ones you're not ready to share.

Make it yours

Pick a theme and an accent color, add your photo, and give buttons icons — an emoji, or the site's own icon fetched for you. Feature the link that matters most and group the rest under headings. Shared in a chat or on social media, your page shows up with a preview card made for you automatically.

Share it — link, QR, paper

Every page comes with a QR code for the link, and a second one that saves your contact card directly onto a phone — no internet needed. found.as also lays out a print-ready sheet of business cards, a "scan to reach me" poster, and an email signature to paste into your mail app.

Want to do more?

Your address can do more than a contact page: send visitors straight to another website, show a short write-up, or hand out a file. It's there if you need it, and easy to ignore if you don't.
