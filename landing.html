How it works

It takes about a minute. Choose your web address — found.as/yourname — pick a password, add your links, and you're online. Want to change something later? Come back with your password and edit anytime. Just don't lose it: there's no way to reset it, so keep it somewhere safe.

What you can add

Add the ways people already reach you: your phone, your email, your website, and the messaging and social apps you use every day, like WhatsApp and Instagram. You don't need to know how links work — type your username or paste whatever you have, and found.as turns it into a tidy button. Then drag them into any order, rename them, or hide the ones you're not ready to share.

Share it as a link or a QR code

Send people the link, or use the QR code that comes with every page — save it or print it on a business card, and anyone can scan it to reach you in a tap. Your page looks right on any phone.

Want to do more?

Your address can do more than a contact page: send visitors straight to another website, show a short write-up, or hand out a file. It's there if you need it, and easy to ignore if you don't.

A simpler, privacy-friendly link in bio

If you've seen link-in-bio pages before, found.as works the same way — one address that holds all your links — without the account, the ads, or the tracking. Put it in your Instagram or TikTok bio, your email signature, or anywhere people look for you.
